An interactive walkthrough of skip-gram Word2Vec

Watch a word embedding learn, one update at a time

A computer can't compare words directly, but it can compare lists of numbers. Word2Vec gives every word its own short list of numbers (an embedding) and adjusts them until words that appear in similar places get similar lists. On this page we train a tiny model right in the browser and look at every step of it, with every number worked out.

The moving parts

These names show up in the code and in the math. We don't need to memorize them; each one is explained again where we use it.

pair
A center word and one word next to it. All training is done one pair at a time.
E (words × D)
The embeddings. One row of D numbers per word. This is what we want in the end.
h
The center word's row of E, copied out. It is the model's current "meaning" of that word.
W (D × words)
A second table, one column per word, used to score how well each word fits next to the center word.
scores = h @ W
One number per word. Bigger means "more likely to be a neighbour".
softmax
Turns the scores into chances between 0% and 100% that add up to 100%.
loss = −ln(p)
How wrong the guess was: 0 if the real neighbour got 100%, larger the smaller its chance.
gradient, LEARNING_RATE
Which way to nudge every number to lower the loss, and how big a nudge to take.
